Lead story · Anthropic

Claude Code Projects turns a goal into parallel cloud coding threads

Anthropic's redesigned Projects (beta in Claude Code) adds a coordinator chat that scopes a goal, spins up parallel threads — each a full Claude Code cloud session on its own git branch — runs tests, opens PRs, and reports back. Shared project memory builds over time; you can steer from your phone and work continues after you step away. Rolling out this week to more Pro and Max cloud-session users; local threads and Team/Enterprise come later.

Source: Anthropic · claude.com

The Wire

Safety

Nadella wants an 'emergency brake' on AI models

In a Saturday X post, Microsoft's CEO said it's time to assess AI's "trust architecture": separate the model from its harness, externalize safeguards, log every meaningful action with tamper-proof evidence, and always let an authorized person pause or shut a model mid-task — "like an emergency brake."

— TechCrunch · techcrunch.com
Product

Google Playground builds browser games from text prompts

Google Labs launched an experimental US-only (18+) platform at playground.google: describe a game, tweak physics and characters in chat, then share a link or publish to a gallery. Powered by Gemini and other Google models; free with higher limits on Google One. Unity Spark (closed beta soon) will add pro-level 3D mechanics.

— Google · blog.google
Finland

Helsinki's Comparables.ai raises $6M seed for AI M&A intel

Pragmatech and Araya led the round for the platform that maps private-company markets with data on 370M+ firms — financials, ownership, valuations, deals and contacts. Used by 50,000+ pros in 30+ countries; founders include Aalto/University of Helsinki ML researcher Muhammad Ammad-ud-din.
