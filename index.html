Lead story · Google

Gemini 4 Argon goes public — a frontier model at a fifth of the price

A day after Meta's Muse Wonder, Google opened Gemini 4 Argon to everyone on 13 Oct, after weeks limited to cyber defenders. It's in the Gemini API and AI Studio, the Gemini app and AI Mode for Pro and Ultra, and in Google's Antigravity coding agent for Ultra only. Google claims it beats GPT-6 Astra, Claude Fable 5.1 and Opus 5.5 across coding, cyber, science, finance and law, and it ranks #1 on the Vals Index. Intro pricing is $2/$10 per million input/output tokens (about a fifth of Astra and Fable, per The Verge) until 30 Nov, then $4/$20, with a 1M unified token limit.

Source: Google · blog.google

Dev tools

Cursor joins 'The X Subscription' — Claude and GPT now cost extra

SpaceXAI bundled Grok, Grok Bot, Cursor and X Premium into one plan ($8 Plus, $30 Premium, $100 Super, $200 Ultra) with a weekly Grok usage pool shared across Grok and Cursor. Third-party models stay available on Premium and up, but at API list price from the first request via top-ups. Existing Cursor plans keep their included usage until you switch.

— The Kernel Wire · thekernelwire.com
Safety

Anthropic says its distillation defences now work — and names Zhipu and Moonshot

Preserved thinking blocks new API accounts from editing prior context, a two-stage classifier (<0.1% false positives) catches reasoning-extraction tricks, and repeat offenders get banned — tens of thousands of accounts so far. Anthropic says Zhipu distilled US models' cyber skills for GLM-5.3. The trade-off: some integrations had to change, and blocked requests are billed again.

— Anthropic · anthropic.com
Outage

Codex had an eight-hour wobble yesterday — check your agent runs

OpenAI's status page logged degraded GPT-6.1 Sol performance in Codex Web, the Codex API, CLI and VS Code extension plus ChatGPT Work from 11:10 to 19:36 Helsinki time on 13 Oct. No root cause yet. If a task timed out in that window, check what actually landed before re-running it.

— OpenTools · opentools.ai
Finland

Finland's Foreign Ministry pilots UMAI, an AI assistant for classified data

UMAI went into pilot use on 13 Oct for a small group of users, running in an environment approved for TL IV classified material. With Valtori, the ministry is a pilot for a shared central-government AI platform; Minister Elina Valtonen framed it as Europe keeping control of critical technology.

— Helsinki Times · helsinkitimes.fi
