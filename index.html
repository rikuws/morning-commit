Alibaba ships Qwen4-Max: a 1M-context flagship at $1.50 / $4.50, with open Qwen4 weights promised

Out of a five-day preview, the first full Qwen4 model is a new MoE with native vision (text, image and video in) and is now on the pay-as-you-go QwenCloud API. Alibaba says it beats Qwen3.8-Max with up to about 50% fewer tokens and over 60% lower average cost per task, and shows it ahead of Claude Opus 5.5 on some tests such as DeepSWE. Artificial Analysis puts it at 43 on its Intelligence Index, below Fable 5.2 and Gemini 4 Carbon but above Haiku 5.5. Smaller open-weight Qwen4 models are due later.

AI FOR SCIENCE

OpenAI opens a science blog with a GPT-6 Astra-assisted lattice Standard Model proof

The first guest post, by UC Santa Barbara physicist Nathaniel Craig, describes a candidate nonperturbative lattice definition of the Standard Model. Craig says Astra turned his strategy into a construction and led the detailed proof. He calls it a beginning: open questions such as reflection positivity remain, and outside experts have not yet endorsed it. Published Sunday.

SECURITY

Old Bitly links in Burger King, McDonald's and Trump tweets were re-registered and hijacked

Short links from 2011-era tweets were registered again on Sunday and pointed at jarring pages such as Wikipedia's list of Nazi concentration camps; one quote post passed 2 million views within hours. Bitly confirmed an issue that "allowed bad actors to repurpose certain old, inactive links", suspended the accounts and is disabling the links. Of 97 Bitly links in Trump's 2009–2013 tweets, 56 were created after the tweets they sit in.

— Ransomnews · ransomnews.com
SECURITY

GPT-5.6 Sol found a one-click universal XSS in uBlock Origin

A malicious site could leak the extension's internal ID, embed uBO's element picker in an invisible iframe and trick a click into adding a trusted filter, which can run JavaScript on any site. It hit the Firefox MV2 version, with a milder variant in uBO Lite, and is now fixed. The researcher verified the bug himself and says Claude wrote most of the proof-of-concept code.

— iter.ca · iter.ca
FINLAND

Helsinki's Intergrid raises €23.5M for an "autopilot" that runs factory energy systems

The round is co-led by Nordic industrial-tech fund Kvanted and state investor Tesi, with Lifeline Ventures and True Ventures joining. Autopilot runs at 100+ sites (customers include Snellman, Atria, Lindström and Vatajankoski) and sits ML predictions on top of rule-based logic, with agents for monitoring, so operators supervise rather than press buttons.
