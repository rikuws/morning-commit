AI R&D

METR: Anthropic is improving models ~2× faster — and AI is doing most of the accelerating

After an 11-week high-access look inside Anthropic, METR estimates capabilities on well-specified tasks are growing at about twice the March 2024–Oct 2025 rate (AECI 1.9×, 95% CI 1.5–2.8×). They guess a majority (~80%) of today's acceleration comes from AI used in R&D, not faster compute scaling — and they saw no clear bottlenecks that would stop it compounding. Anthropic redacted much of the most informative evidence.

Source: METR · metr.org

OPENAI

GPT-6 Cyber ships for Daybreak Red: 48.5% ExploitGym, 81 novel Firefox bugs

OpenAI's new cyber model scores 48.5% on ExploitGym (up from 32.1% for GPT-5.6 Cyber) and stays in-scope on 99.93–100% of honeypot tests. It helped produce the 948 Linux LPEs and 81 previously unknown high-severity Firefox vulns Mozilla has been patching. Initially API/Codex for approved Daybreak Red customers only; classified Critical under the Preparedness Framework.

— OpenAI · openai.com
GOOGLE

Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash: native 15-second video, conversational edits, 30% cheaper

Google DeepMind's video model is generally available on the Gemini API and Cloud. Clips go to 15s (from 10s), with multi-turn conversational editing, stronger reference adherence, and $0.07/s for 720p — 30% below Omni 1.1. Draft at 360p/720p, then upscale to 1080p or 4K; outputs carry SynthID.

— Google · blog.google

Beta rolls out this week. Connect banks and cards through Plaid; Claude sees balances, credit limits, and up to two years of transactions — it cannot move or invest money. Hub chats aren't used for training; Anthropic pitches it as a first step toward working alongside licensed advisors.
