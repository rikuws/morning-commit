MARKETS · ANTHROPIC

Anthropic prices its IPO at $83 a share and starts trading on Nasdaq today, the biggest IPO on record

The offering is 980 million shares, about 956 million sold by Anthropic itself and the rest by existing holders. AP says the price values the Claude maker at about $1.5 trillion and passes SpaceX as the largest IPO ever; Renaissance Capital puts gross proceeds at about $81.3 billion. Shares are expected to trade under the ticker ANT. The pricing came in above expectations despite bubble worries and the turmoil after Nvidia's CEO change.

The Wire

MODELS

Google's Gemini 4 Neon: a fast frontier model at $3 / $15 with a 1M context

Google says Neon is #3 overall on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (61, at xhigh thinking) and #2 on the Vals Index, with its lowest fabrication rate yet in internal Antigravity testing. Cached input is $0.15 per million tokens. It is rolling out in the Gemini API, Antigravity and the Gemini app, and it's already in Cursor and GitHub Copilot.

— Google · blog.google
CODING AGENTS

Mistral Vibe goes desktop, with local and cloud agents on Git worktrees

The macOS and Windows app (Linux 'coming soon') combines Vibe CLI's local runs with cloud sandbox sessions, so parallel agents can work on separate branches of the same repo. It works with VS Code, JetBrains and Zed via ACP, plus MCP. European developers get three free months of Pro ($14.99/mo) with the code LECHONK, and Mistral had to add more codes after day one.

— AI Weekly · aiweekly.co
CHIPS

OpenAI shows how its models redesigned parts of its Jalapeño chip, and now AMD GPU blocks

One multiplier redesign used about 15% less cell area and cut latency from two clock cycles to one; an adder lost about 15% of its area with the same latency. OpenAI is now applying the approach to AMD hardware and working with Synopsys to build frontier models into the chip-design (EDA) tools engineers already use.

— OpenAI · openai.com
FINLAND · SLUSH

Slush opens in Helsinki, and Stubb pitches Finland as an AI-infrastructure superpower

12,000 people at a sold-out Slush on 18–19 Nov. In the opening talk the president pointed to clean energy (96% of supply, he said), Nokia's networks, and IQM, Bluefors, Iceye and Reorbit, and suggested that Google's planned €13B investment in Finland could end up much larger.

— ETN · etn.fi
