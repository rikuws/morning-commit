AGENTS

OpenAI launches ChatGPT Wallet, so agents can pay without ever seeing your card

Built with American Express, Mastercard and Visa, the wallet lets you add a card once and then approve each agent purchase with a security check such as Face ID or a fingerprint, after reviewing the items, total and card. The payment network hands out a token in place of the real card details, and existing rewards and dispute protections still apply. It starts with ChatGPT Pro subscribers in the US, through dots, alongside tools and guidance for businesses that want to accept agent payments.

FUNDING

LangChain raises a $300M Series C at a $3B valuation

CapitalG led the round, with GIC, DFJ Growth, IVP and Sapphire Ventures joining. The open-source library from 2022 has turned into an agent-management business: LangSmith pitches itself as model- and cloud-neutral, with customers such as Harvey, Rippling, Clay, Nvidia, Toyota and Salesforce.

— TechCrunch · techcrunch.com
ROBOTICS

OpenAI disbands its stand-alone robotics team

Robotics researchers move into the main research org under Mark Chen, and applied robotics and hardware staff into Applied under Greg Brockman. OpenAI says it is betting on general-purpose models driving robots directly, not retreating. Its booth at the CoRL robotics conference in Austin this week sat empty.

— Fortune · fortune.com
AGENTS

Perplexity brings Teammates, its always-on agents, to the Mac

Teammates are given an ongoing responsibility, such as triaging bug reports, tracking metrics or running a growth campaign, then plan the work, bring in more agents as it grows and report back for review. Previously on iOS and the web, it needs a Perplexity Max subscription.

— 9to5Mac · 9to5mac.com
FINLAND

Peter Sarlin's PostScriptum takes a majority stake in Finnish AI consultancy Brillian

Founded in 2024, Brillian does AI deployment end to end, from strategy to everyday use, from two Finnish hubs, and earns most of its revenue abroad with clients such as Siemens, Storytel and Carnival Cruise Lines. The founders and staff keep a substantial minority, and the plan is bigger projects and new AI hubs next year.
