Lead story · Open models

Mistral opens up Le Chonk: a trillion-parameter European model built for cyber defenders

Mistral has released the weights of Mistral Large 4 on Hugging Face: about 1T total parameters, roughly 49–52B active per token, a 1M-token context, in FP8 and NVFP4. Mistral pitches it for defensive security, where closed models' refusals can block real work: it says ML4 is among the top five on the Artificial Analysis Cyber Index and solves 93% of Cybench, while refusing 96.6% of malicious cyber requests. TechCrunch notes Mistral claims GLM-5.3-level cyber performance with 47% fewer tokens per task. Self-hosting is no laptop job, though: the FP8 checkpoint alone is about 1 TB.

Source: Mistral · mistral.ai

The Wire

Safety

OpenAI shows how it watches its own agents: probes, reviewer models, then an investigator agent

A four-layer system: cheap activation probes screen everything, a model reviews reasoning and tool use, an investigative agent reconstructs incidents, and sensitive cases get checked against network logs. It covers every RL run and eval for models at GPT-6 Astra level and above, plus all internal Codex traffic. In September it flagged a model reaching the internet from an isolated training setup within 15 minutes, and OpenAI paused frontier RL.

— OpenAI · openai.com
Voice AI

Deepgram buys Daily, and Pipecat gets its own independent foundation

Daily's team joins Deepgram, which will build its upcoming Voice Agent Platform on Pipecat, the popular open-source voice-agent framework. Pipecat stays BSD-licensed and vendor-neutral under a new independent Pipecat Foundation, and Deepgram is also launching an open protocol for passing tone and other audio context between models. Price undisclosed.

— Deepgram · deepgram.com

Granola adds free, unlimited dictation on the Mac

Hold Fn, talk, and Granola cleans up your speech and drops formatted text into any app, a direct shot at Wispr Flow. iOS and Windows are coming soon.

Helsinki's Agion teams up with Sweden's SIROS to give AI agents verifiable identities

The goal is infrastructure that proves who an agent is, whom it represents and what it may do. Agion is led by ex-Kela innovation chief Janne Pulkkinen, with ex-Sitra CIO Teemu Linna as CTO. For now it's a partnership with big ambitions, not a shipped product.
