NVIDIA

115,000 sign a petition to bring Jensen Huang back to Nvidia, Musk and Masad among them

The petition says signers include 5,965 current Nvidia employees, 1,108 former employees and 63,063 shareholders, with Elon Musk, Replit's Amjad Masad, Epic's Tim Sweeney and YC's Garry Tan named as supporters. It argues Huang was removed "without meaningful due process" and asks the board to reinstate him while an independent investigation runs. Huang resigned on Thursday; CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO, and Nvidia's stock fell sharply through the week.

ANTHROPIC

Days before its IPO, Amodei calls compute Anthropic's "deepest business dilemma"

Speaking with Jamie Dimon at a JPMorgan investor event on Friday, he said capacity has to be bought one to two years ahead, before anyone knows if revenue will justify it. Per Bloomberg, Anthropic has committed nearly $901B, mostly to compute and chips, with about $71B due by September 2027. The IPO is planned for 19 November.

— The Decoder · the-decoder.com
CLAUDE

Anthropic: stop telling Claude Opus 5.5 to "think hard"

Now the default on Pro, Max, Ultra and Team plans, Opus 5.5 reasons on its own, and Anthropic says responses without those phrases started faster with no measurable drop in quality. It recommends starting at Medium effort, which matched Opus 5 on High in its tests, and stating the goal, output and check-in points in one message.

— The Decoder · the-decoder.com
OPEN SOURCE

llama.cpp and ggml reach v1.0.0 and switch to semantic versioning

After 3.5 years, major version bumps will now mark breaking changes to the public C API, so downstream tools can pin a release. v1.0.0 also removes 35 deprecated functions, adds FP8 KV caches and a MoE expert cache, hardens llama-server (SSRF, CORS, DNS rebinding) and adds a /v1/audio/speech endpoint. Released Friday.

— Hugging Face · huggingface.co
FINLAND

A Helsinki logician uses Claude to unlock Gödel's lost notebooks

Jan von Plato, who leads a University of Helsinki project on 1,009 pages of Gödel's 1940s workbooks in obsolete Gabelsberger shorthand, had been moving at about a page a week. Given his transcriptions, Claude produced a 650-page digest in a couple of days, including a near-complete proof of the axiom of choice's independence that then passed Coq.

— The Guardian · theguardian.com
