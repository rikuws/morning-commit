GOOGLE WORKSPACE · MCP · AGENTS

Google makes Workspace MCP servers and CLI GA, and from 1 February agents must use them instead of the REST APIs

Google-hosted MCP servers now cover Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Calendar, Chat and Tasks, and a Workspace CLI with packaged agent skills serves terminal agents. The catch: AI agents must use MCP rather than calling Workspace REST APIs directly, with enforcement from 1 February 2027. A free Standard tier keeps published quotas; a paid Scaled tier for higher limits starts billing on 1 April 2027. Admins get agent audit logs, DLP and human-confirmation rules.

Source: Google Workspace Updates · workspaceupdates.googleblog.com

The Wire

MCP · CLAUDE

On MCP's second birthday, servers can ship Skills to Claude, and Tasks and Elicitation go GA

Skills over MCP (public beta in Claude and Claude Code) lets a server publish playbooks next to its tools, loaded only when needed. Tasks let servers run long jobs in the background, and Elicitation lets them pause for user sign-off: Supabase confirms costs before creating a paid project, Railway asks before removing a service. File Uploads is coming soon.

— Anthropic · claude.com
RESEARCH · BIOLOGY

OpenAI says GPT-6 models can design new proteins with only reasoning and code

No specialised biology models generate the designs. An internal research model matched or beat specialised models on many design tasks, and in lab tests with Onava, proteins based on GPT-6.2's designs showed evidence of binding across five targets. Specialised models still helped supply sequences and pick candidates for testing.

— OpenAI · openai.com
EUROPE · FUNDING

The EU plans a €50B Sovereign Tech Equity Fund, mainly for AI and quantum companies

According to the Financial Times, citing two officials, the fund would make larger, riskier and longer-term bets than existing EU schemes, to cut Europe's dependence on US and Chinese tech. Reuters says it could not immediately verify the report.

— Reuters / FT · live.euronext.com
CHIPS · INFERENCE

Amazon buys Decart's AI optimization stack and team, a reported $7.5B bet on Trainium

Amazon is buying the technology behind the Decart Optimization Stack and bringing over its three co-founders and engineers. The stack tunes kernels, compilers and runtimes, and Amazon says it will make training and inference on its own chips faster and cheaper as it competes with Nvidia.

— TechCrunch · techcrunch.com
