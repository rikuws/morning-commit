Lead story · Security

PHP2Shell: AI-found bugs in PHP itself let attackers run code on almost any PHP site

PHP shipped emergency fixes on Wednesday for two use-after-free flaws (CVE-2026-109618 and -109619) that allow unauthenticated remote code execution in the engine, not in any app on top of it. Wiz says every PHP version from the last decade is hit by at least one of them, and it demoed exploits on stock WordPress, Drupal and Joomla images, plus memory leaks that expose passwords and API keys. Calif found one using GPT-5.6 Sol through OpenAI’s Patch the Planet program, and Wiz found both independently with Gemini 4 Argon and GPT-5.6 Sol. Details leaked on Tuesday, which forced an early release. Update to 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36 or 8.2.35; CISA says 8.1 and older should go offline until patched.

Source: Wiz, CISA, OpenAI, BBC · wiz.io

The Wire

Agents

Anthropic opens Claude Duet, a separate app where Claude works as a proactive agent

It flags what needs your attention, keeps working when you step away and checks before acting for you. It’s a waitlisted research preview on iOS, Android and claude.ai, starting with US Pro and Max users, and its background checks count against your plan’s usage limits.

— Anthropic · claude.com
Local models

Recursive’s Flint is a free 40B coding model for your laptop, built largely by its own AI researcher

A mixture-of-experts model with about 2.5B parameters active at a time, Apache 2.0 on Hugging Face. Recursive measured about 131 tokens per second on an M4 Pro MacBook Pro, roughly twice similar models, and says it leads models under 50B on SWE-Bench Verified. It suggests splitting big tasks into small steps.

— TechCrunch · techcrunch.com
Security

OpenAI pitches a playbook for disclosing AI-found bugs at internet scale

Alongside the PHP fix, OpenAI laid out five stages, from fixing upstream to staged early notice for hosts, WAF vendors and governments, and shared a PHP version checker as a Codex skill. It says similar findings will “increase sharply in the coming months” and asks the security community for feedback.

— OpenAI · openai.com
Nordics

Vaisala’s Xweather teams up with Nvidia to stretch lightning warnings from one hour to three

Xweather’s lightning observations are being combined with Nvidia’s StormScope short-range storm model and Earth-2 weather stack, with other severe weather to follow. No launch date, regions or deal value were given.
