Lead story · Security

One hacker, an army of AI agents, 100+ breached company sites

Security firm Norforge found more than 20 exposed panels of Cairn, an open-source “problem-solving engine” that orchestrates AI agents, being used for attacks, and watched over the attackers’ shoulders. The busiest, a Chinese-speaking hacker, gave the agents fewer than 100 Chinese characters of instructions and let them work through 1,200+ sites across the US, Canada, Australia and Japan; Reuters identified break-ins at over 100 companies, including John Deere, SharkNinja and Cummins. Successful intrusions took about 23 hours, the campaign burned roughly $5,000 in tokens (about $50 per breach), and every task ran on a worker labelled claudecode_deepseek-v4.1-flash. Deere says there’s no sign sensitive data was accessed; SharkNinja says only a standalone support site was hit.

Source: The Verge, citing Reuters and Norforge · theverge.com

The Wire

Policy

DHS orders DeepSeek out of every US federal civilian agency, open-weight spin-offs included

A Homeland Security order, signed in the summer and filed publicly on Monday, bars civilian agencies from buying anything from DeepSeek and tells them to remove what they have. The removal also covers “third-party products and services developed using DeepSeek implementations”, so vendors that embed or fine-tune its open weights are caught too. Exceptions need a “compelling justification”.

— CyberScoop · cyberscoop.com
Models

MiniMax M3.1 Flash is the mystery “Space Bunny”, now at $0.15 per million tokens

MiniMax confirmed the anonymous OpenRouter model was an early M3.1 Flash build that pushed more than 200 trillion tokens before anyone knew whose it was. The 428B model takes text, image and video with a 1M context, claims 70% cheaper agent runs and nearly 3× faster inference than M3, and scores 73.7% on DeepSWE (self-reported). Weights are promised later.

— SiliconANGLE · siliconangle.com
Dev tools

Claude Code 2.1.304 adds /auto-mode-setup and fixes a worktree bug that could lose commits

The new guided /auto-mode-setup reads your project and recent sessions to teach auto mode about your environment. Mods can now insist that only a person approves a tool call. Among the long list of fixes: claude --worktree no longer deletes the worktree and its branch without asking when HEAD was detached. Code Review also gets Quick, Standard and Deep levels.

— Anthropic on GitHub · github.com
Nordics

Tandem Health’s AI scribe plugs straight into Apotti

The Swedish company’s assistant is now connected to the Apotti client and patient records system through an API. That makes Apotti the first Finnish records system with an integration like this. It listens to appointments, drafts the notes and suggests diagnosis codes. After pilots in Helsinki’s physio and occupational therapy units, testing is expanding to social services, student healthcare and the Vantaa–Kerava wellbeing county.

— The Intelligent · theintelligent.fi
