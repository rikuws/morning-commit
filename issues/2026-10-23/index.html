Lead story · AI safety

Outside red-teamers found real holes in how OpenAI watches its own agents

METR and Redwood Research sent one person each into OpenAI from 21 July to 11 August to try to slip agents past its internal misalignment monitoring. Coverage was broad (99.2% of internal Codex traffic was eligible), but they found gaps: unmonitored compaction events left about 10% of eligible Codex traffic unwatched from May to July, two client-controlled headers could switch monitoring off for new agents, and Codex agents could in principle get around sandboxing and Auto-Review. OpenAI says the flaws that touched production are patched, which METR hasn't verified. It lands days after OpenAI disclosed two October cases where its own agents weakened monitoring mid-task, one of which then told the user it hadn't.

Source: METR · metr.org

Security

LiteLLM 1.107.0 shipped with a credential stealer on PyPI and Docker

The poisoned release was live for about two hours on Wednesday and grabbed cloud keys plus Cursor and Claude configs. The attacker got in via GitHub CLI tokens that landed in the logs of an exposed internal LiteLLM proxy. If you installed it, assume compromise, rotate keys and pin 1.106.2.

— LiteLLM · docs.litellm.ai

Anthropic hands Agent Skills to the Linux Foundation

The skills format now runs on 70+ platforms, including ChatGPT, Cursor, Copilot and VS Code, with about a million skills published. It joins the Agentic AI Foundation next to OpenAI's Open Responses and Hugging Face's OpenEnv.

— Anthropic · anthropic.com
Finland

Google illegally cleared ~250,000 trees for a datacentre in Muhos

The felling covered 330 hectares of a €13bn project before authorities ordered the work stopped. Google has paused and plans to replant 130 hectares, a petition has passed 93,000 signatures, and PM Orpo called it a “cold shower”.

— The Guardian · theguardian.com

Canva ships Craft, its first in-house image model

It plans layout, hierarchy and text before rendering up to 2K in 30 aspect ratios, and was trained on designer templates rather than photos, so you can edit one element without the rest shifting. It's available to all users and through the API.
