Lead story · Security

OpenCode breach leaks saved provider keys — and strangers ran up the bills overnight

Someone got into “a legacy system that we were in the process of decommissioning” at OpenCode late Friday, exposing the OpenAI, Anthropic and Google keys that some paid Console users had saved in their workspaces. OpenCode says 1.4% of Console users were hit (a subset with external providers linked), emailed them Saturday morning and tied it to “a large scale distillation attack”, without explaining how. Users were seeing charges before any announcement: one reported $281 on OpenAI and $168 on Anthropic from 2 a.m. ET, others $700–1,700. If you ever linked a key in Console, revoke it at the provider (not in Console), check usage since 16 Oct and put a spend cap on the new one. Local CLI keys aren’t reported as affected.

Source: The Latent · thelatent.co

The Wire

Open weights

Reflection ships Beam: a 501B coding model that’s actually Apache 2.0

Beam is a sparse MoE with 23B active parameters and a 1M-token context, released in BF16, FP8 and NVFP4 with day-zero vLLM, SGLang and Transformers support. It comes with Mirror, an open CLI harness (Python front end, Rust agent core) it was trained alongside, and also runs in Pi, OpenCode and Hermes. 40+ partners host it, from Ollama and OpenRouter to Dell and HPE for air-gapped installs.

— Reflection · reflection.ai
Work

Anthropic’s own data: fewer juniors, more meetings, humans as the bottleneck

Junior technical hires fell from 27% to 9% of hiring since early 2025, while code commits per engineer are 5x what they were six months ago — with no rise in reverted agent-written code. Interactive Claude sessions sit idle about 80% of the time waiting for a human, and the median employee spends 20% more time in meetings than a year ago.

— Anthropic · anthropic.com

DeepSeek swaps in V4.1-Flash-1017 — no code change needed

The `deepseek-flash` API name now serves the new checkpoint, and the retired v4-flash names route to it at the Flash price. Briefings report it scores 77.0 on DeepSWE v1.1 versus 74.0 for Claude Opus 5 but trails well behind on HLE without tools; DeepSeek also opened standalone Search and Fetch APIs for agents.

Finland

Minister Ikonen: the public sector can’t stay stuck in AI pilots

The goal is 20% higher public-sector productivity through AI and automation by 2031, with €10M set aside for an AI-agent and automation programme in 2026–27. Valtori is building a shared state AI platform, and a new strategic AI steering group brings the state, municipalities and wellbeing areas together. Her biggest risk for Finland: “that we leave it undone.”
