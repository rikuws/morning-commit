Google ships a Gemini agent that plans work and picks its own model

At Gemini at Work, Google Cloud launched a universal work agent that takes objectives, uses tools and skills, and connects to Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack, Jira, Git, BigQuery and MCP servers.
