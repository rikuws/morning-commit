AI × MATHS

An unreleased Anthropic model just proved a quantum-computing conjecture open since 2008

Physical Superintelligence (PSI) says its in-house research system, running a non-public Anthropic model, proved the Aaronson–Ambainis conjecture: on random inputs, quantum computers can't be exponentially faster without a rare hidden pattern. Lean accepted the proof on day three of the run, and the system then improved the result twice. The Lean code is public; Stanford researchers who settled a special case in August call it "a huge result" and are writing an expert exposition.

Source: PSI · psi.inc

CODEX

Codex 0.167 adds an agent command center for your background agents

Run `codex agents --no-daemon` to see every agent, with unread markers on finished results. You can also switch backends from the session picker without losing drafts, keep chats visible across model providers, preview local images while composing, and expand patch details while an approval prompt is open.

AGENTS

Perplexity launches Teammates: always-on agents that own ongoing work

Each Teammate gets its own thread, runs jobs in parallel, builds on Perplexity Computer's projects, skills and memory, and can be reached in Slack. It joins OpenAI's Dots and Claude Duet in the 24/7 agent race, but needs the $200/month Max plan. Srinivas pictures hundreds of teammates per person.

— The Verge · theverge.com
FINLAND

Helsinki tightens the rules for big data centres

New city principles require operators to bring new low-emission, non-combustion power online as their demand grows, recover waste heat at scale for district heating, prefer non-forested sites and follow Finnish labour law. The forested Henriksdal site faces resident protest and a threatened moss species, so alternatives are now being studied.

— Datacenter Nyheter / HS · datacenternyheter.se
