Lead story · Cryptography

Claude cracks the 25-year riddle of the NIST curve seeds: “It’s just timestamps”

The NSA-supplied seeds behind P-192 through P-521 were never a hash of a forgotten joke phrase. Anthropic says an internal research model worked out in under half an hour that they’re output from the old C `lrand48` generator, seeded with Unix timestamps from May 1997 to April 1998 — P-256 is candidate 1,842 of a run started 16 Aug 1997, 01:52:34 UTC. It spotted the generator’s fingerprint (top bits always zero), recovered states with LLL lattice reduction, and even explained six base points via Arjen Lenstra’s FreeLIP. Not a break: it makes a backdoor far less plausible. Filippo Valsorda re-implemented it in Go to verify; the $36,864 bounty goes to ISRG (Let’s Encrypt). B-163 and the P-256 base point remain unexplained.

Source: Anthropic · anthropic.com

The Wire

Research

Same day, second Anthropic paper: Claude out-designs published quantum circuits

Given plain-language specs and an automated scorer, Claude Mythos 5 found circuits beating the best published designs on eight primitives. Highlights: the first reversible divsteps modular inversion with no stored branch history (verified in Lean), and a Reed–Solomon decoder about 12x leaner than Google’s for decoded quantum interferometry. Anthropic notes it had to nudge the model to stop playing safe.

— Anthropic · anthropic.com
Dev tools

Replit’s CEO says it dropped Anthropic — insiders say the deal stands

On stage at TechCrunch Disrupt, Amjad Masad said Replit moved on over cost, partnership and its own benchmarks: “I think ChatGPT is better now.” A person familiar told TechCrunch the partnership is still in place, and Replit’s late-September production notes still listed Claude models. Replit added OpenAI as a provider in August.

Agents

GPT-6 Pro in ChatGPT now splits work across parallel agents

Pro users get delegated sub-tasks running at once in the Chat tab, with per-agent progress and reviewable results. Separately, OpenAI’s Tibo says Codex and Work stretch usage further by reusing context across subagents, caching longer and routing for cache hits. No latency, cost or quota numbers were published.

— Runtime Wire · runtimewire.com

Elisa pilots a real-time Moontalk AIRI that joins business calls

The Oulu-built assistant (Moontalk is part of the Elisa group) used to record calls and write summaries; the new beta, now with selected business customers, pulls from a company’s own sources and suggests answers during the call. It runs on Nvidia Nemotron models with distributed edge compute.
