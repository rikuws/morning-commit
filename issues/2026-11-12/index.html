DEV TOOLS

Today is the day OpenAI's models are due to leave Cursor

OpenAI proposed 12 November as the end of its contract supplying models to Cursor, after SpaceX's $60B purchase of Anysphere, and it is withholding future models such as GPT-6 Astra. The date is still described as a proposal and talks continue. Cursor's Truell says OpenAI models are about 5% of traffic; Claude, Gemini, Grok and Composer stay. To keep OpenAI models, bring your own API key or use the Codex extension, but that works only in local Chat and Agent, not Tab, Auto, cloud agents, the CLI or the SDK.

Source: OpenAI Help Center · help.openai.com

The Wire

SCIENCE

DeepMind's AlphaGenome 2 finds nearly twice as many gene-changing DNA variants

The new model predicts DNA effects one cell type or state at a time, and can condition on a few dozen cells of fresh single-cell data without retraining. On an immune-cell benchmark it recovered 30% of causal variants vs 16% before. Free for non-commercial research via the API; skills for Antigravity are coming.

— Google DeepMind · deepmind.google
AGENTS

Databricks turns Genie into an always-on agent with subagents and an inbox

The new Genie takes ownership of long-running work, delegates to subagents, tracks progress and asks humans for decisions through a priority inbox and email, inside Unity Catalog controls. Genie and Genie Ontology are GA; the always-on mode and Genie Search are in preview.

— Databricks · databricks.com
CHIPS

TSMC will invest up to $1B in Anthropic and OpenAI

TSMC's board approved up to $500M in Anthropic common shares at the IPO price and up to $500M in OpenAI preferred stock, tying the world's biggest chip foundry more tightly to two of its largest end customers.

— CNA · channelnewsasia.com
NORDIC

Helsinki's Switchpoint Bio raises $5.3M to read drug effects from a blood sample

The Helsinki and Cambridge, Mass. biotech combines targeted DNA-methylation profiling, single-cell data and AI to track how diseases and treatments change cells. $5M of the pre-seed comes from Treehouse Delmar Investments; the money goes to clinical validation and growing the team in both cities.

— STT Info · sttinfo.fi
