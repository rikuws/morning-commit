Anthropic's first day: a 34% pop, a 17% close, and Asia sells off anyway

ANT opened at $111.50 on Nasdaq, briefly valuing Anthropic above $2 trillion, then gave back much of it to close at $97.17, still 17% above the $83 offer (about $1.76 trillion). It slipped another 6% after hours. The Nasdaq fell over 2% to a seven-month low, and on Friday morning Asian markets dropped hard: the Taiex fell more than 6.8%, the Kospi 5.4% and the Nikkei as much as 4.9%, on worries that the AI boom has peaked.

AGENTS IN PRODUCTION

OpenAI's Performance Factory: Codex agents that investigate, fix and verify ChatGPT slowdowns

A performance alert opens a task, an agent investigates and proposes a fix for an engineer to review, and after the deploy another agent checks that latency is back to baseline. The process came out of a shared `perf-forensics` skill, tested with evals built from past regressions. It's a useful blueprint for anyone turning agent skills into workflows that run on their own.

— OpenAI · openai.com
COMPUTER USE

Gemini can now drive your Mac in a background window

The Gemini app for Mac can now work across local files, native apps and the web to finish multi-step tasks. It runs in an isolated window with a live preview in the chat, so your own cursor stays free. You pick which apps it can touch and approve key actions. For now it's US-only, English-only, and needs a Google AI Plus, Pro or Ultra plan.

— Google · blog.google
WORLD MODELS

Genie 4 opens to developers in the Gemini API and AI Studio

One day after launch, developers can generate continuous 720p, 24fps worlds with native audio from a text prompt or an image, then move around with keyboard controls and change the weather, lighting or events with live prompts. Google pitches it for prototyping interactive experiences and for training robots and agents.

— Google · blog.google
TAMPERE · SLUSH

Tampere brings 27 startup and research teams to Slush investors

Under the Next from Tampere banner, startups and Tampere University Research to Business teams pitched across four themes: industrial tech, semiconductors, health tech and AI. The projects include ways to assess whether organs are viable for transplant and to predict breast cancer metastasis. The aim is more research-based, student-founded growth companies.
