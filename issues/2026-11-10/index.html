NEW MODELS

Claude Fable 5.2 is out, and in testing it ran 1,000+ subagents for eight days to build a bootable OS

Anthropic shipped Fable 5.2 and its gated twin Mythos 5.2 on Monday. They are the same model: Fable has cyber and bio safeguards and is open to everyone, while Mythos goes to vetted security and life-sciences groups. Anthropic says outputs come up to 30% faster than 5.1, and the price drops to $8/$40 per million tokens. Its showcase: from an empty folder, Fable coordinated over 1,000 subagents for eight days and produced an operating system that boots on a laptop, with drivers, file storage, windows and a login screen.

Source: Anthropic · anthropic.com

The Wire

MODELS

Meta's Muse Ember: a $0.10-per-million-token model for the work agents repeat all day

Meta Superintelligence Labs' smallest Muse model is built for tool calls, coding subtasks, routing, extraction and long-document parsing, and reads images and audio too. Meta pitches it as the volume worker under its bigger Muse Wonder: "Muse Wonder plans, Muse Ember handles the volume."

— TechCrunch · techcrunch.com
PRIVACY

Google confirms people review some Gmail and Drive content connected to personal Gemini

A Hacker News thread on Monday flagged Google's Connected Apps help page: a subset of that data, summaries and excerpts of emails and files, is read by human reviewers, and for a short file the excerpt may be the whole thing. Work and school accounts are excluded. Disconnecting Workspace and turning off Keep Activity stops it going forward.

— Google Gemini Help · support.google.com
SECURITY

Fireworks AI widens its breach disclosure to customer datasets and unfinished model weights

An update dated 9 November says the attackers, linked to an "LLMjacking" group that resells stolen model access, also reached account and billing data and, for some customers, secrets, datasets and incomplete weights. Fireworks says inference and training SDK were not hit, and has paused self-serve dataset uploads and downloads.

— Fireworks AI · fireworks.ai
FINLAND

Japan's MUFG joins Helsinki GPU cloud Verda's $189M unicorn round

MUFG's venture arm completed a strategic investment in the former DataCrunch, citing demand for AI compute and Europe's push for sovereign AI infrastructure. Verda says it now runs about 250 people and will use MUFG to grow in Japan and across Asia-Pacific.

— Fintech Observer · fintechobserver.com
