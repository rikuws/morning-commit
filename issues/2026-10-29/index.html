Lead story · Models

Gemini 4 Carbon takes #1 on Artificial Analysis, but cyber defenders get it first

Google's new frontier model lands just two weeks after Gemini 4 Argon. It scores 59 on Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5, the top spot, and Google reports state-of-the-art security numbers measured without guardrails: 88.2% on SEC-bench Pro for finding real vulnerabilities, 54.8% on ExploitGym and 71.0% on CWE-bench for patches. Google also says cheating on Terminal-Bench 4.0 dropped to 0.0%. The catch: only a subset of cyber defenders get access this week, then trusted partners through the Fairwind programme, while Google joins US pre-release testing. Developer pricing is set at $2/$10 per million tokens to start, rising to $4/$20.

Source: Google · blog.google

Dev tools

GitHub Copilot Fusion picks and combines models for you

Now generally available: choose Fusion and an effort level (Standard, High, Max), and Copilot routes each prompt to one model, cascades from a cheap draft to a stronger model, or has one model critique another. GitHub says Fusion Max came within two points of Claude Code with Opus 5.5 Max on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index at about half the cost per task. In VS Code, JetBrains, the CLI and the Copilot app, with 30% off for a limited time.

— GitHub Changelog · github.blog
Enterprise

Anthropic gives Claude Enterprise admins reports on what the tokens actually bought

Now generally available: smart reports analyse sampled team transcripts to show workstreams, deliverables, spend per stream, friction points and recommendations. Pooled group budgets and in-app surveys ship too. Context from TechCrunch: Jellyfish data shows AI coding tool spend up 10x this year, now about 2% of engineering cost.

— Claude · claude.com
Open models

Nvidia's Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe: open weights, 40 languages, speaker labels

A free, open-weight speech model for recordings up to an hour, with transcription, translation, speaker attribution and timestamps, and about 4B active parameters. It's for recorded audio, not live streams; Nvidia has a separate small streaming model for that.

— TechCrunch · techcrunch.com
Nordics

Nordic and Baltic media, Medialiitto included, set three terms for AI companies

Fourteen publisher groups and journalist unions from eight countries want consent before journalism is used commercially in AI, disclosure of training content (or a presumption that it was used), and an arbitration-style way to settle disputes, since talks with AI firms haven't produced satisfying deals.

— Medialiitto · medialiitto.fi
