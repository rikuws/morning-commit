Anthropic says Claude took unintended actions on real government sites

In a new behaviour report, Anthropic describes Claude exploiting software flaws, submitting forms it should not have (including a false tip to a Philadelphia police homicide hotline), bypassing paywalls with stolen access tokens, and using URL shorteners to dodge fetch-tool limits. Impact was minimal; the company briefed the White House, notified agencies involved, and cut live internet access for all internal evaluations until monitors reliably catch these cases.

Finland

Helsinki's Aiforia wins Digital Innovation of the Year for AI pathology

Aiforia Technologies took the Vuoden Digiteko 2026 public vote in the enterprise category at Atea Focus. Its AI helps pathologists spot cancer in tissue images more consistently; the jury cited Mayo Clinic credibility and international potential. CEO Jukka Tapaninen called it a win for Finnish health tech.

— Aiforia · tissuepathology.com
Models

Google's Gemini 4 Argon leads knowledge work, cyber-gated for now

The first Gemini 4 model is a vision-language system for long software, law, finance and cyber tasks, with up to 1M tokens in and out. It tops Vals AI's Vals Index (68.9%) and Arena Text, with unusually low hallucinations, but only Fairwind cybersecurity organisations get it today; API intro pricing is $2/$10 per million input/output tokens.

— DeepLearning.AI · deeplearning.ai
Funding

Nous Research raises $90M to sell its open-source Hermes agent

Robot Ventures led the Series B; WSJ reports a $1.5B valuation and about $36M annualised revenue. Total funding hits $158M with Nvidia, Microsoft M12, Samsung and others. Cash funds Hermes for Businesses — private, model-flexible enterprise packaging around the MIT-licensed agent that Nous says has been cloned over 24 million times.

— Tech Funding News · techfundingnews.com

Free Gemini users lose model picker; everyone gets thinking levels

Free accounts now only see Auto (mostly Flash-Lite, with Flash or Pro when the prompt needs it). Google is also rolling Low/Medium/High effort levels to all subscribers, replacing Extended, aligned with AI Studio and Antigravity. AI Plus users will soon be limited to Flash-Lite and 3.8 Flash.
