MONEY

OpenAI cuts its funding target from $30B to $20B after a tepid response

OpenAI had been reported to be raising $30B at a $1.4T valuation, then at $1.25T, ahead of a planned IPO. The FT now says the target is up to $20B, led by investors from the UAE, with a later retail offering for wealthy individuals to fill the gap. The WSJ says $11B is secured so far and some large investors were tepid. It lands in a rough week for AI money: big tech stocks sold off over infrastructure spending, and Anthropic's planned IPO faces questions too.

WEB

One robots.txt line to opt out of AI training at OpenAI, Google, Microsoft and Anthropic

An IETF draft from Anthropic's Kevin Kelley adds a policy token, __usage_ai-training, that sites use like a user agent to say no to training. Anthropic has started, OpenAI aims for the coming months, Google for 2027 and Microsoft from 1 Feb 2027. It's a preference, not a crawl ban.

— The Next Web · thenextweb.com
AGENTS

A Claude agent picked a gene therapy and injected it into a mouse brain, then refused to go on

At Exin Therapeutics, a Sonnet 5 agent chose the drug, brain region and coordinates and drove a motorized rig. After a dropped connection it spotted a target outside the rig's range and refused to continue; it was right, and the team fixed the code by hand. Opus 5 and 5.5 declined the job entirely. Not peer-reviewed yet.

— The Decoder · the-decoder.com

Claude Code 2.1.316 adds subagent effort control and fixes a restricted-mode upload gap

New CLAUDE_CODE_SUBAGENT_EFFORT sets the effort subagents run at, plus layered policies and pooled group budgets for the Claude apps gateway. --restricted sessions could upload files outside the working dirs; fixed. Opus 4.8 fast mode goes away on 30 November.

APPLE

Apple's CEO: "We don't want to ship AI for AI's sake"

John Ternus told Wired's Steven Levy he likes where Apple is on AI and that people shouldn't have to think about whether AI or ordinary software did the job. Asked whether agents could make apps unnecessary, he said making products is a deeply human thing.
